# STAR Story Bank

## Story 1: Python Automation (Campus Safety Solutions)
**Use for:** Technical problem-solving | Process improvement | Initiative

**S:** At Campus Safety Solutions, our team was manually remediating configuration errors across 100+ endpoints with no audit trail.
**T:** Eliminate the manual steps and make the process repeatable and verifiable.
**A:** Wrote Python scripts that automated REST API calls to each endpoint, ran validation checks, and logged every action to a centralized audit trail. Didn't wait to be asked — saw the gap, built the solution, tested it on a subset before rollout.
**R:** Resolution time dropped ~60%. Zero manual errors after rollout. The audit trail became the team standard.
**Reflection:** The highest-leverage thing in a support role isn't closing tickets faster — it's eliminating the ticket class entirely.

## Story 2: Knowledge Base (IT Support Center)
**Use for:** Reducing repeat work | Documentation | Scaling yourself

**S:** Seeing the same questions week after week — same SaaS login issues, same SSO problems. 50+ tickets a week, many duplicates.
**T:** Cut repeat ticket volume without adding headcount.
**A:** Built a structured Confluence knowledge base — runbooks, onboarding guides, step-by-step troubleshooting docs. Published it, communicated it to the team, iterated based on what was still coming in.
**R:** Repeat incident volume dropped. Users started self-resolving common issues. Freed up time for harder escalations.
**Reflection:** One good runbook can replace 20 future tickets.

## Story 3: SLA Compliance (Alara Logistics)
**Use for:** Operational ownership | Sustained performance | Process design

**S:** Joined Alara as IT Manager with no consistent incident management process. Escalations were ad hoc, post-incident docs nonexistent.
**T:** Build a system that sustains 100% SLA compliance across 50+ devices.
**A:** Designed incident management workflows with clear escalation paths and RCA templates. Formalized access management for onboarding and offboarding. Set up monitoring and network protection protocols.
**R:** 100% SLA compliance over 12 months. Zero critical security events. Zero access gaps during staff transitions.
**Reflection:** Reliability is designed, not accidental. The difference is documentation and process.

## Story 4: SevensCleaners (Solo AI Platform)
**Use for:** End-to-end ownership | AI fluency | Learning by building

**S:** Wanted to prove I could build with LLM APIs at a production level, not just use them.
**T:** Design and ship a full-stack AI service dispatch marketplace, solo, from zero.
**A:** Built SevensCleaners — integrated Claude and LLM APIs for intelligent contractor matching, automated dispatch routing, and customer communications. Designed prompt engineering workflows. Managed full cloud deployment myself.
**R:** Live in production. Zero downtime on customer-facing transactions. Solo delivery, no team, no budget.
**Reflection:** The best way to demonstrate AI fluency is to ship something real.

## Story 5: Client Ownership (DJEZE Events)
**Use for:** Client relationships | Business ownership | High-stakes delivery

**S:** Founded DJEZE Events in 2019 and ran it solo — client acquisition, vendor coordination, day-of production, financial reconciliation, everything.
**T:** Deliver live events consistently with budgets up to $15K and zero margin for error.
**A:** Built repeatable systems for pre-event planning, vendor management, and client communication. Handled paid media, logistics, and real-time problem-solving on-site.
**R:** 50+ events. Repeat clients and referrals. Sustained the business 5+ years alongside school and work.
**Reflection:** Running your own thing teaches you to own outcomes, not just tasks.

## Story 6: Executive Communication (Campus Safety Documentation)
**Use for:** Non-technical stakeholder comms | Translating technical data | Influencing decisions

**S:** Leadership needed to make FY2027 budget decisions about endpoint infrastructure. They weren't technical and the data was in raw system logs.
**T:** Translate endpoint lifecycle data into something leadership could act on.
**A:** Built structured lifecycle assessment documentation — pulled the data, organized it into a clear narrative with actionable recommendations in plain language.
**R:** Findings fed directly into FY2027 budget planning. Leadership had a clear picture of what to replace, when, and why.
**Reflection:** Technical work only creates value if decision-makers understand it.
