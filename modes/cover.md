# /career-ops cover — Cover Letter Mode

Read cv.md, config/profile.yml, and the JD. Detect archetype.

## Structure

P1 - Hook: Why this company specifically. 2-3 sentences. Specific, not generic.
P2 - Business/client proof: Most relevant client-ownership or business-impact story for this archetype.
P3 - Technical depth: Technical proof that matches the JD stack.
P4 - Close: Bridge technical and non-technical. Reference documentation or leadership comms proof.

## Rules

- Max 350 words
- No em dashes
- No "I am passionate about" or "I am excited to"
- Contractions fine
- Prose only, no bullet points
- Honest about gaps — don't pretend missing skills aren't missing

## Output

Save to output/Ugwueze_Eze_[Company]_CoverLetter.md
